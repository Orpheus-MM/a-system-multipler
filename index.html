Operating model

Make learning
a continuous loop.

Morning: retrieve

Collect customer evidence, product changes, research, incidents, and open decisions. Separate source evidence from interpretation.

Midday: challenge in parallel

Use independent AI roles. Ask what is unsupported, what could fail, who is excluded, and which system constraint matters.

Afternoon: prototype risk

Build for learning, not completeness. Test behavior, state, permission, comprehension, or feasibility.

Evening: decide and record

Document decision, evidence, tradeoff, outcome, and lesson. A decision trail demonstrates senior judgment.

Overnight: inspect safely

Run read-only audits, comparisons, clustering, and update briefs. Require citations, uncertainty, and human-review boundaries.

Friday: improve system

Compare intent with outcome. Repair prompts and workflows. Turn only stable, repeated methods into skills.
