Dev Bar capability radar

weekly, not daily

Inspect

Understand change

Capture what changed, intended job, design relevance, current workflow fit, adoption cost, permissions, and one bounded test.

Classify

Choose a posture

Adopt when proven. Test with one real task. Watch without commitment. Ignore when no current use case exists.

Measure

Demand evidence

Compare time, quality, failure modes, human judgment required, and repeatability before normalizing a tool.

Triage score

0–2 per dimension

8–10

Act or decide

High relevance, urgency, leverage, evidence, and genuine ownership. Put it in today's constrained brief.

5–7

Schedule or delegate

Important, but not immediate. Assign a real owner and review date rather than keeping it mentally active.

0–4

Watch or archive

Include 3–4 in weekly synthesis. Archive 0–2. Direct requests from accountable leaders always receive human review.
